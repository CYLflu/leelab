KNU Zoonotic Virus Lab

We study how animal viruses cross species barriers and emerge as new pathogens in humans, with a focus on influenza A virus and SARS-CoV-2.

By understanding how and when cross-species transmission occurs, and what factors allow a virus to become stably established in a new host, we aim to better mitigate the impact of future pandemics caused by zoonotic diseases.

What we do

Understanding cross-species virus transmission

The spillover of animal viruses into new hosts drives the emergence of new human pathogens. We investigate the mechanisms and drivers behind these events.

Cross-Species Transmission

Investigating how and when animal viruses such as influenza A virus spill over into new hosts, including humans.

Host Adaptation

Identifying the viral and host factors that allow a virus to replicate and transmit stably within a new host species.

Pandemic Preparedness

Translating insight on zoonotic viruses into strategies that mitigate the impact of future pandemics.
